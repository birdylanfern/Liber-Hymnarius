# Common of Apostles: printed rubric verification

Source: Liber Hymnarius (1983), PDF scan spread 140 (printed p. 266), and spread 142 (printed pp. 270-271).

1. Printed p. 266 states under **AD I VESPERAS**: *Hymnus Exsultet, ut ad II Vesperas, p. 270.* The site's taxonomy was missing I Vespers. Added the slot and an alias to existing `gb-16685.gabc`, *Exsultet caelum laudibus*. This is a documented cross-reference, not a new GABC file.
2. Printed p. 270 has *Exsultet caelum laudibus* under **AD II VESPERAS**, for non-Eastertide use. The existing file's `in aliis temporibus` variant remains unchanged.
3. Printed p. 270-271 has a different hymn, **Tristes erant Apostoli**, under **Tempore paschali**. This must not be filled with the `Exsultet` score. The expected title is displayed in the website as a source-identified GABC gap.
4. Printed p. 266 shows three Invitatory melodies (modes III, IV and Eastertide). Those existing alternatives were not changed.
