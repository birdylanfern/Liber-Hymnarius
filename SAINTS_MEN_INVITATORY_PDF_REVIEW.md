# PDF review: Common of Holy Men, Invitatory

The 1983 Liber Hymnarius, printed p. 311 (PDF image page 162), places **Mirabilem in sanctis suis Dominum** under **Commune Sanctorum Virorum / Ad Invitatorium**. The score in `gb-20882.gabc` had previously been classified under the Common of Virgins (`c-virgines`), which does not match the printed heading.

Correction: set `category: c-viri`, `hour: inv`, and `variant: mirabilem in sanctis` for `gb-20882.gabc`. The first printed setting is the non-Eastertide form; the separate Eastertide form also printed on p. 311 has **not** been inferred from this score. The original musical notation is unchanged. This verification concerns the rubric and incipit, not note-by-note comparison.
