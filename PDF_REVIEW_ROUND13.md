# Round 13 — Ambiguous variants made visible

The 60 ambiguous variant records are now clearly labelled VARIANT UNCHECKED in the hymn browser when an hour has multiple defined liturgical variants and the source does not specify which one. Such entries are grouped under a separate VARIANS INCERTA heading instead of being presented as an established Sunday, weekday, nocturnal or daytime assignment.

This is a display and provenance correction, **not** a verification of the underlying chants. The 238/425 slot count remains unchanged. All 451 source GABC files are preserved.
