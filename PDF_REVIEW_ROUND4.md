# PDF review round 4 — taxonomy and variants

This pass fixes category definitions that contradicted the headings visible in the supplied scanned *Liber Hymnarius* (1983).

- **Good Friday (printed p. 67):** add the Invitatorium slot. The score records already had `hour: inv` but the site taxonomy omitted that hour.
- **Holy Saturday (printed p. 69):** add the Invitatorium slot. This does not imply a matching local score is present.
- **Common of the Blessed Virgin Mary (printed pp. 258–260):** add explicit Vespers slots for solemnities, feasts, and memorials. Three already classified *Ave maris stella* GABC scores receive corresponding variant metadata.

The changes preserve all original musical notes, all provenance labels, and existing UNCHECKED flags. They do not automatically assign other scores on the basis of page proximity. The printed book has additional rubrics not yet reflected in the site taxonomy, so this is a focused correction, not a claim of full verification.
