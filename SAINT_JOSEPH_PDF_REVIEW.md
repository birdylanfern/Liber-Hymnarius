# St Joseph: source-verified Vespers placement

Source: *Liber Hymnarius* (1983), printed p. 355, PDF scan page 184.

The heading **Die 19 martii / S. Ioseph Sponsi Beatae Mariae Virginis / Ad I et II Vesperas** immediately precedes **Te Ioseph celebrent**. The existing transcription `gabc/hymns/gregobase-liber/gb-20562.gabc` already has category `s-03-19` and printed page 355 but lacked an hour. It is now assigned to the website's `v` slot, which represents the printed I and II Vespers use for this feast. This confirms the liturgical placement, not note-for-note musical identity.

Rebuilt index and reconciliation: **333/429** populated, **96** unfilled. No new GABC files were added, and no provisional filler was created.

Other St Joseph slots remain unfilled until the proper score can be identified: the printed invitatory **Laudemus Deum nostrum in solemnitate beati Ioseph** (p. 357) and Lauds hymn **Caelitum Ioseph** (p. 358). Do not use the unrelated Benedictine `Laudemus Deum nostrum` transcription (GregoBase 10451, p. 556) for this invitatory.
