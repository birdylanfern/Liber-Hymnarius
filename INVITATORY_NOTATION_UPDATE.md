# Invitatory psalm notation update

Invitatory detail pages now offer a manual selector for three existing complete Psalm 94 GABC tones (IV*, V, VI). Selecting a tone renders the notation directly beneath the antiphon and includes it in the GABC source and player. The choice is not automatic and is clearly labelled UNCHECKED PAIRING; the correct psalm tone for each antiphon has not been established. The original text-only Psalm 94 remains visible. No chant GABC files were modified. Populated-slot coverage remains 334/429.
