# Venite pairing reconciliation — Round 6

All 49 records in `data/invitatory-mode-catalogue.csv` now have an explicit record in `data/venite-printed-pairings.csv` and a matching `VERIFIED_VENITE_REFERENCES` runtime mapping in `app.js`.

New references:

- **Hodie scietis** (24 December): antiphon printed p. 10 (PDF p. 12); its continuation on printed p. 11 explicitly refers to *Venite* p. 168 (Tone E).
- **Populus Domini**, alternative GregoBase transcription gb-14205: same Saturday Ordinary Time antiphon as gb-15600, printed p. 180 (PDF p. 97), referencing Tone D p. 165. The transcriptions are not identical; their notes have not been compared.
- **Fontem sapientiae**, provisional reused placement: Tone IV, p. 138, inherited from gb-20870.
- **Mirabilem in sanctis**, provisional reused placement: Tone IV, p. 138, inherited from gb-20882.
- **Regem Apostolorum**, provisional reused placement: Tone III, p. 135, inherited from gb-10398. Other valid printed melody variants are separately represented.

**Scope:** complete coverage of the *49 inventoried records*, not proof that all antiphons in the full book are present, nor that every GABC note matches the print. Provisional reuse remains provisional.
