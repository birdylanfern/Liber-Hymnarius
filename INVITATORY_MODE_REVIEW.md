# Invitatory antiphon and Psalm 94 tone review

This pass inventories the modes of all indexed invitatory antiphons and compares them to the three local complete Psalm 94 scores (IV*, V and VI). The site now suggests a same-mode score only when the antiphon mode matches one of those three options. It **does not** automatically attach the score, mark the pairing as verified, or hide the UNCHECKED warning. The printed book may require a different melodic formula or a special invitatory tone; a matching mode alone is insufficient evidence.

See `data/invitatory-mode-review.json` for every antiphon, its mode, and candidate status. To regenerate, run `node tools/build-invitatory-mode-review.js`.
