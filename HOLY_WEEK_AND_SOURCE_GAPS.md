# Source-backed reconciliation: Holy Week and missing seasonal scores

## Corrected

- **Holy Week, Vespers:** *Vexilla regis prodeunt* (printed p. 58). The PDF prints this hymn under `II. Hebdomada Sancta / Ad Vesperas`. The project already contained the corresponding GregoBase GABC (`gb-19794.gabc`), indexed to printed p. 58 but previously categorized under Lent. A separate liturgical-use reference now appears in the Holy Week Vespers slot. The original file is unchanged. This verifies the placement, not every musical note.

## Confirmed missing scores (not substituted)

- **Advent after 16 December, Office of Readings:** *Veni redemptor gentium*, printed p. 11. No matching GABC in the local catalogue.
- **Lent, Sunday Vespers:** *Audi benigne Conditor*, printed p. 48. No matching GABC in the local catalogue.
- **Holy Week, Office of Readings:** *Pange lingua gloriosi proelium certaminis*, printed p. 61. The catalogue does not contain a matching score for this placement.
- **Holy Saturday, Office of Readings:** *Christe caelorum Domine*, printed p. 69. Not to be confused with other hymns with similar incipits.
- **Holy Saturday, Invitatory:** *Christum Dominum pro nobis passum et sepultum*, printed p. 69. The Lent antiphon `Christum Dominum pro nobis tentatum` is different and must not be reused.

The counts measure populated catalogue slots, not fully verified musical transcriptions. Unresolved slots are left unfilled rather than receiving unrelated scores.
