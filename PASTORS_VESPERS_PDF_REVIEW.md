# Common of Pastors: II Vespers PDF verification

Reference: *Liber Hymnarius* (1983), printed pp. 297-299 (PDF scan pages 155-156).

- **Vir celse forma fulgida**, p. 297, is explicitly under **AD II VESPERAS / Pro uno pastore**. The book prints alternate fourth stanzas for pope, bishop and priest on p. 298. Both existing GABC transcriptions (gb-17944 and gb-17945) are assigned to the single-pastor Vespers slot. They remain distinct musical transcriptions; neither was overwritten.
- **Sacrata nobis gaudia**, p. 299, is explicitly under **Pro pluribus pastoribus**. Existing gb-17949 is assigned to the plural-pastors Vespers slot.

This corrects metadata, not musical notes. No note-by-note verification was performed. The catalogue reconciliation now reports 330/429 populated slots (previously 328/429). This is coverage, not a count of fully verified chants.
