# PDF review round 5 — seasonal taxonomy corrections

Based on the scanned Liber Hymnarius (1983):

- Printed p. 26 explicitly labels *O lux beata caelitum* as **Sanctae Familiae — Ad I et II Vesperas**. Reclassified gb-14250 from generic Christmas to Holy Family and assigned hour `v12`.
- Printed pp. 31–33 label the Mother of God Vespers as **Ad I et II Vesperas** and include an **Ad Invitatorium** on p. 33. The taxonomy now contains that invitatory slot (the existing score gb-8699 already had the correct metadata).
- The generic seasonal taxonomy previously introduced a standalone `Hora media` slot in addition to `Tertia`, `Sexta`, and `Nona`. The printed seasonal sections provide the three individual midday hours, not a separate fourth hymn for `Hora media`. Removed this artificial generic slot. Other explicit Hora media rubrics remain unchanged.

No musical notes were changed. Existing provenance and UNCHECKED markers are preserved. This is a targeted taxonomy correction, not a full note-by-note verification.
